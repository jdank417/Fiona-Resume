Lesson Plans

Math · Grade 5 Sept 30, 2026

Multi-Digit Multiplication with the Standard Algorithm

Students become “Math Detectives,” estimating first, then applying the standard algorithm to larger factors while attending to place value.

Standard
NY-5.NBT.5
Objective
Accurately multiply multi-digit numbers, align place values, regroup, and use estimation to check reasonableness.
Differentiation
Place value charts, algorithm support tool, flexible grouping, error-analysis extensions.
Assessment
Independent practice, exit ticket, thumbs self-assessment

ELA · Grade 5 Oct 7, 2026

Making a Claim Using Evidence from Pumpkin Jack

An interactive read-aloud with purposeful stopping points, where students collect evidence on a class anchor chart and make a claim about what will happen to the class pumpkin.

Standard
NYS 5R1
Objective
Use relevant details and evidence from the text to make a logical claim.
Differentiation
Sentence frames, verbal rehearsal, teacher conferencing, Evidence Detective Challenge.
Assessment
Claim & Evidence Sheet, exit ticket, self-assessment

Science · Grade 3 Nov 16, 2026

Social Animals: How Living in Groups Helps Survival

Students become “Animal Behavior Detectives,” investigating why meerkats live in groups and tigers live alone, then building an evidence-based argument.

Standard
NYS 3-LS2-1
Objective
Identify whether animals live in groups or alone and use evidence to explain how that behavior helps them survive.
Differentiation
3 levels of organizers, info cards, and exit tickets; sentence frames; riddle enrichment.
Assessment
Graphic organizer, differentiated exit ticket
