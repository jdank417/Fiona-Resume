Reflective Commentary by My Cooperating Teacher

“

Whether teaching a reading lesson or a photosynthesis lesson in science, Fiona is a hard working, passionate and conscientious teacher. She developed many lessons which she taught in succession over an 8 week-long period. She has high expectations and learned how to scaffold assignments for the levels in our class. Her precision to detail and effective procedural breakdown allow students to grasp the multi step math lessons. The students enjoyed her lessons and made marked progress in Module 1, multiplication and division, in math.

In all of my observations, Fiona embraced every student's questions and needs to the best of her ability. She is a very intuitive and natural teacher.

— Christine Beck, 5th Grade Cooperating Teacher, Minnesauke Elementary School
